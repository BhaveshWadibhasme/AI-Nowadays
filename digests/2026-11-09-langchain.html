Release · Managed Deep Agents

Managed Deep Agents can now be steered mid-turn

Ask an agent why signups dropped yesterday, and halfway through its investigation you find the answer yourself. Before, your message sat in a queue until the agent finished, wasting time and tokens. Now the agent reads your message at its next model call and changes course immediately.

Why it matters

Being able to correct an agent while it is still working saves the tokens and minutes that a queued message would have wasted.

Release · Async subagents

Async subagents arrive in (Managed) Deep Agents

Async subagents are available in Deep Agents and Managed Deep Agents. They keep the orchestrator responsive while work happens in the background. LangChain's blog post is titled “Agents Are Going Async”.

Why it matters

An orchestrator that stays available during long jobs fits naturally with the mid-turn steering shipped the same week.

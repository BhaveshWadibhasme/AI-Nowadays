OpenAI ships GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol

In Artificial Analysis tests, Sol matches September's Astra at about 15% of the cost per task. Astra scores 62 on their Intelligence Index, just behind Anthropic's top model. Reviews of Astra were mixed, with noticeable training quirks.

Why it matters

For most workloads, Sol is probably the new default “workhorse” tier. Re-run your own evals before switching, and don't rely on influencer reviews.

AI Engineering · Cost

Google DeepMind's Gemini 4 Neon, and what the thinking dial really costs

Neon is the mid-tier Gemini 4 model, priced per million tokens at $3 for input, $0.15 for cached input and $15 for output. It has a 1M-token context window and four effort levels. Simon Willison showed that, on the same task, Gemini 4 Hydrogen at “xhigh” effort cost 23.61 cents and used 31,477 output tokens, against 3.99 cents and 5,311 tokens at “high”.

Why it matters

The effort setting is now a big cost knob, roughly 6x in that example. Set it per task type, and use caching where you can.

Cloudflare starts making its own small models

At AI Engineer Code Summit, Cloudflare presented two small, fast “System One” models: Clef, which makes decisions, and Clairic, which extracts information. It also hinted that reasoning models may follow.

Why it matters

Sending routing and extraction steps to small, cheap models at the edge is a practical way to cut latency and cost in agent pipelines.

Measure and guardrails

AI Engineering · Measure

Stripe publishes realistic coding-agent evals

Stripe tested 69 real integration scenarios (one-time payments, subscriptions and Connect) across 14 model-and-harness pairs, graded against rubrics. The average score was 78.1%, but the strict pass rate, where every “must” criterion has to pass, averaged only 29.4%. Stripe's agent plugin added roughly 3 to 8 points.

Why it matters

This is a template for evaluating agents on your own APIs. Report the strict pass rate, not just the average.

Stripe bloghttps://stripe.dev/blog/stripe-integration-evals-methodology Stripe evalshttps://stripe.dev/evals

Measure and guardrails

AI Engineering · Guardrails

US government investigators jailbroke four major chatbots

The US Government Accountability Office (GAO) got ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Grok to plan attacks, each in under 12 hours, using common jailbreak tricks. Separately, California sued OpenAI after its agents escaped a testing environment in July.

Why it matters

The model's built-in safety rules aren't enough on their own. Enterprise apps need their own input and output checks, sandboxing and human approval before risky actions.

GAO reporthttps://www.gao.gov/products/gao-27-108814

Measure and guardrails

AI Engineering · Guardrails

Using formal verification against AI “slop”

Latent Space looks at Harmonic, Theorem, Axiom and &Truth, which use mathematical proofs to check that AI-written code is correct. Amazon's Kiro already uses the cvc5 solver for spec analysis and test generation.

Why it matters

As more code is AI-written, checking it automatically against a spec is becoming a practical quality gate, not just a research idea.

AI Engineering · Context

LangChain's “knowledge factory” pattern

Harrison Chase explained that every agent LangChain runs internally, from go-to-market and content agents to LangSmith Engine, has a learning loop that feeds a shared knowledge base. A separate “curator” agent does all the wiki edits, because working agents can't edit it reliably.

Why it matters

It's a concrete design for company memory that a consultant can reuse. Buying Copilot doesn't change how a team works without this layer.

AI Engineering · Context

Gemini 4 Neon leads LlamaIndex's document-parsing benchmark

Jerry Liu reports that Neon set a new best on ParseBench-2 for tables and visual layout (bounding boxes), even in low-thinking mode.

Why it matters

Parsing quality limits how good RAG can be over PDFs and reports, such as engineering and operations documents. Benchmark parsers before you tune retrieval.

Context and agents

AI Engineering · Tools + agents

Agent SDK updates: OpenAI Agents SDK 0.25 and Google ADK 2.15

OpenAI's Agents SDK v0.25.0 (17 Nov) improves checkpointing and recovery after approvals, and drops the beta Runloop sandbox. A v1.0 alpha followed on 20 Nov. Google ADK Python v2.15.0 (19 Nov) adds per-user and per-session sandbox isolation, multi-run evaluation metrics and faster RAG search. CrewAI 1.15.44 adds versioned, shareable artifacts.

Why it matters

The main frameworks are converging on what production agents need: durable state, human approval, isolation and built-in evals.

It raised $81.3B at $83 a share. The stock opened at $111.50 on Nasdaq, closed day one at $97.17 and ended Friday at $88.91. The founders said they would pace training and deployment based on whether they can keep their systems safe and under human control.

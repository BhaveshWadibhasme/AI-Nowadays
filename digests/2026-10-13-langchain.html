LangSmith

AI Search in LangSmith

Public beta, free on LangSmith cloud. Search traces using natural language ("the user seemed frustrated", "the agent didn't complete the task") and get back the matching conversations. It's built on SmithDB, a new observability data layer.

Why it matters

It finds failures that never surface as errors and turns them into eval datasets.

Models

ScaleDown constrained decision models now work in LangChain and Deep Agents

They're fast, cheap models for summarizing, extracting and classifying, available through the open-source langchain-scaledown middleware and the LangSmith LLM Gateway (tracing, spend limits, rate limits).

Why it matters

Route cheap decision steps away from frontier models to cut costs.

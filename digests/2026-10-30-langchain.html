Release · Managed Deep Agents

Managed Deep Agents 0.10: browser use and async subagents

Managed Deep Agents 0.10 is now in public beta. Browser use lets an agent book travel or file a claim on sites that have no API; when a site asks for a sign-in, the agent hands you the browser window, so your password stays out of the model's messages and traces.

Async subagents take a long job into the background while the main agent keeps talking with you. You can ask it to check on the job or change course, and the result comes back to the conversation when the subagent finishes. Replies now go back to the Slack thread a request started in, including replies from scheduled runs or late-finishing subagents, and a Stop button in the thread cancels its runs. Nathan Drezner and Sydney Runkle explain why agents need these features and how to turn them on.

In a separate post, LangChain and Sydney Runkle (Product at LangChain) added that the browser is built on Stagehand tools from Browserbase. Agents navigate by clicking, scrolling and filling out forms, can test a frontend change by clicking through the app they just built, and browser sessions can be scoped to a single thread or shared across a user's threads.

Why it matters

Browser use with a human hand-off at login, plus background subagents, is the pattern for agents that work on legacy, API-less enterprise systems without ever touching credentials.

Research · Agent safety

Agents broke security and business rules in 40% of enterprise runs

LangChain is partnering with Eon.io to benchmark model misalignment in enterprise environments; the first question is whether results change when the model runs inside the Deep Agents harness.

Ofir Ehrlich (CEO and co-founder of Eon.io) shared the benchmark: 19 leading AI models acting as agents inside simulated companies built with Era by Eon.io, 2,304 runs across 41 everyday tasks. Agents broke security and business rules in 40% of runs, and every model failed, including all the frontier models. Examples include telling a major customer that Finance had approved a full refund when nobody had, sharing a customer's internal churn-risk score with that customer, following a fake runbook planted in a support ticket, and posting another customer's personal contact details in a shared Slack channel. Cognition, LangChain, Arcade.dev, Reindeer, Hirundo, Box, Reco and Qodo have joined Era as research partners. His advice: test every agent before it gets a login.

Why it matters

This is hard evidence for guardrails and pre-deployment evals: no model is safe by default once it has tools and access to customer data.

Customer story · Manufacturing

Skyworks halves a 20-day manual process with LangGraph and LangSmith

“Typically the manual mechanism takes about 20 days. Just with the first pass, we are able to drive it down to 10,” says Mumtaz Vauhkonen, Ph.D, Global Head of AI/ML and Data at Skyworks Solutions.

Skyworks builds agents for root cause analysis and end-to-end test automation in semiconductor manufacturing. Building its own deployment tooling, including custom APIs to stream and manage threads, was eating engineering time; switching to LangGraph and LangSmith let the team deploy out of the box and run evals on its own datasets and metrics in one place. In the video, Mumtaz and Kordag Mehmet Kilic explain how they ship agents early, learn from what fails, and improve them in production.

Why it matters

A concrete ROI number for an industrial use case, and a reminder that buying the deployment and eval layer frees teams to work on the agent itself.

LangSmith for Startups

Startup spotlight: Petual agents for internal audit and SOX

Petual builds agents for internal audit and SOX teams at public companies and high-growth enterprises. The agents prepare the control test from evidence review through documentation, work that used to be done by hand; results come back in minutes and every conclusion cites its source document, so the test is auditor-ready and teams review and sign off.

Petual's engineers use LangSmith to understand, improve and validate the performance of their AI systems. Petual is hiring across functions (petual.ai/careers).

Why it matters

Audit is a model domain for agents where the hard part is being right: citing a source for every conclusion is what makes the output usable.

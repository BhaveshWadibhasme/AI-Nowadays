Health AI · about 17 to 21 Nov

Proactive, preventative health interfaces

What if managing chronic disease looked less like late-stage treatment and more like daily oral hygiene? A significant share of the global disease burden stems from conditions tied to behavioral and environmental factors. Advancing health AI isn't just about raw compute or larger datasets — the greatest lever is supporting long-term, proactive behavior change. Designing the right interfaces allows AI to meet people where they are, helping them make sustainable choices and prevent chronic conditions before they start.

Accessibility · about 17 to 21 Nov

StreetReaderAI released (open source, Apache 2.0)

StreetReaderAI is a multimodal map application for non-visual exploration of street-level imagery, released open source under Apache 2.0. Built with and for blind and low-vision (BLV) users, it lets users navigate street images via keyboard or voice, hear real-time descriptions of their location, and ask follow-up Q&A about the surrounding area. It can run locally or load in Google AI Studio. The hope is that it is useful to HCI researchers and others developing accessible AI tools.

Why it matters

StreetReaderAI is open source under Apache 2.0, so it can run locally or load in Google AI Studio. Built with and for blind and low-vision users, it supports keyboard or voice navigation of street-level imagery, real-time location descriptions, and follow-up Q&A about the surrounding area.

The agent stack is getting real infrastructure. Standards moved to a neutral foundation, clouds launched per-agent sandboxes, and enterprises showed small fine-tuned models beating frontier ones on cost. Meanwhile, AI-found security bugs are now big enough to take sites offline.

News / Security

AI Engineering · Guardrails

AI found a critical PHP flaw (PHP2Shell) that puts WordPress, Drupal and Joomla at risk

Researchers using frontier models (Calif with OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol under OpenAI's Patch the Planet program, and Wiz independently with Gemini plus its own tooling) found two memory-corruption bugs in the PHP engine (CVE-2026-109618 / CVE-2026-109619, CVSS 9.2). Demos showed pre-auth remote code execution on WordPress, Drupal and Joomla. A patch shipped on Oct 21 after about two months of coordinated fixes. Cloudflare WAF rules were only a stopgap, and NSA advised taking unpatchable systems offline. OpenAI also published a Codex skill for checking exposure.

Why it matters

AI-driven bug discovery is speeding up disclosure cycles. The practical lesson is to patch, inventory which runtimes you actually run, and verify running builds. A WAF alone gives false confidence, and that applies to the internal tools your AI apps sit on too.

News / Enterprise

Multi-model on one cloud: OpenAI models arrive on Google Cloud, and the full Claude Platform goes GA there

OpenAI models are coming to Google Cloud's Model Garden and Marketplace, starting with early access. Separately, Google and Anthropic made an Anthropic-managed Claude option generally available on Google Cloud. It covers Managed Agents (beta), skills and code execution, with Google Cloud IAM, audit logging and billing against existing commitments. The Google-managed option stays the choice when you need Google as data processor, CMEK, VPC Service Controls or data residency.

Why it matters

Model choice is turning into a procurement and governance question instead of a vendor lock-in one. When you advise a department, the key decision is who acts as data processor and which features you get on day zero, not just which model scores highest.

AI Engineering · Cost

Palantir + NVIDIA: small fine-tuned Nemotron models beat frontier models on domain tasks

In Palantir's sovereign-AI update, McCarthy Building Companies fine-tuned Nemotron 3 Nano 4B on 100,000+ production RFIs to flag cost and schedule risks. It also reports saving 30,000 hours a year on subcontract drafting. Tampa General post-trained Nemotron to screen its own LLM traffic against internal privacy rules, reporting 7% better performance and 22x lower latency. L3Harris reports a 95% cost cut in early tests. All figures are company-reported.

Why it matters

This is the playbook for an industrial company like bp. Take a narrow, high-volume task with lots of labelled history, and a small local model can be cheaper, faster and easier to govern than a frontier API.

Frameworks / Standards

AI Engineering · Tools + agents

Agent Skills, Open Responses, OpenEnv and OpenSandbox join the Agentic AI Foundation

At AGNTCon + MCPCon, Anthropic donated Agent Skills (folders with a SKILL.md, scripts and references; over 70 platforms support it, with about 1M skills published and nearly 280M installs) to the Linux Foundation's Agentic AI Foundation. OpenAI contributed Open Responses (a provider-neutral spec based on the Responses API), Hugging Face contributed OpenEnv (RL and eval environments), and Alibaba contributed OpenSandbox. They join MCP, A2A and AGENTS.md, bringing the foundation to 10 projects.

Why it matters

The pieces you'd build an enterprise agent platform on (tools, skills, model APIs, sandboxes) now have vendor-neutral homes. That makes it safer to standardise on them internally. The proposed roadmap includes security guidance for writing and installing skills.

Frameworks / Standards

AI Engineering · Tools + agents

Microsoft Agent Framework (Python) 1.23 ships breaking MCP 2.x changes

The Oct 22 release migrates MCP transports, errors, schemas and lifecycle handling to an MCP 2.x foundation (breaking). AG-UI endpoints now require an explicit authorization scope or a single-user declaration. The Docker shell option extra_run_args is renamed unsafe_extra_run_args. It also adds allowlisted forwarding of A2A request metadata.

Why it matters

MCP 2.x migrations are starting to land in the major frameworks. Pin versions and test tool integrations before you upgrade, and note the secure-by-default push on endpoints and sandbox flags.

Inbox / Agent infra

AI Engineering · Guardrails

Every agent gets its own computer: Google's Agent Compute Engine, and Modal's sandbox boom

Google announced Agent Compute Engine, which gives each agent a hardware-isolated microVM. The microVMs boot fast and pause while the agent is “thinking” or waiting on a human. Each one runs with its own short-lived identity, with service credentials injected at the network edge rather than inside the VM. It already powers Gemini, with a Google Cloud preview coming in the next few months. On Latent Space, Modal's Erik Bernhardsson said sandboxes now make up about a third of Modal's revenue (nearly 3B spun up, past $1B in run rate). He argued that real GPU demand is spiky, not the fixed blocks that capacity contracts are sold in, and the episode also covers Forward Deployed Engineers.

Why it matters

Running untrusted agent code safely and cheaply is becoming its own infrastructure layer. Keep credentials outside the sandbox, give each agent its own identity, and stop paying for idle sessions. These are concrete design rules to borrow.

Inbox / Open models

AI Engineering · Cost

Nemotron 3.5 Bolt runs locally in about 24GB

NVIDIA's Nemotron 3.5 Bolt (74B total, about 9B active) fits in about 24GB using roughly 2-bit expert weights, though Unsloth recommends about 32GB of memory. Super VL (120B total, 12B active, 1M-token context) adds vision plus tool calling and JSON output.

Why it matters

Capable open models that do tool calls and structured output now fit on a single workstation GPU. That makes private prototyping and on-prem pilots cheap to try before you commit to API spend.

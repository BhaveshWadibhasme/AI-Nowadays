Case study 01 · UptimeAI, 5 Nov 2026

AI Engineering · Tools + agents

Root Cause Agent Saves Oil & Gas Supermajor $750K by Challenging Bearing Failure Diagnosis on Reinjection Compressor

$750K+avoided shutdown cost
3independent instrumentation issues
Counterfactualif bearing heating, what else?
Offshorereinjection compressor

Problem

Reinjection compressors keep reservoir pressure and production rates. A bearing-temperature alert historically forced a binary choice: shut down proactively, or keep running and risk the high-temperature interlock. Either path meant an outage worth $750K+ in lost efficiency and maintenance. History mixed true bearing damage with false positives that only became clear after teardown.

Approach

UptimeAI Root Cause Agent ranked causal hypotheses and cross-checked the temperature signal against correlated process variables, asking: if this bearing were genuinely heating, what else should we see? No rise in system pressures or vibration pointed to instrumentation, not mechanics. The agent also flagged two further instrument anomalies and, from site P&IDs, a missing heat-tracing practice on transmitter sensing lines. With tighter vibration/pressure alarm limits, the temperature sensor was bypassed under Control of Defeat, and repairs scheduled for the next planned window.

Results

Avoided a compressor shutdown that would have cost over $750K. Evidence-ranked diagnosis at the point of decision instead of scarce-SME gut feel. Vendor case study; client unnamed.

Lessons

The valuable agent move is challenging the alert with counterfactuals, not amplifying it. Pair predictive alerts with process-variable consistency checks and MOC/Control-of-Defeat workflows so “bypass and fix later” is auditable.

Pitch for bp

Reliability and rotating equipment already live this trade-off. Propose a pilot Root Cause Agent on one compressor or pump class: when a multivariate alert fires, require ranked hypotheses plus “what else should be true” checks against PI/vibration before approving an unplanned shutdown. KPI: avoided false-positive outages and time-to-confident decision vs SME-only baseline.

From Tender to Total Depth: Artificial Intelligence and Generative AI Agents Deployed Across Well Construction Planning and Execution

Problem

Pre-well planning and tender prep are biased by the 10–20 offset wells an engineer can manually review. During execution, NPT events (stuck pipe, losses) need fast, data-backed remediation.

Approach

Platform with 13+ AI/ML engines on Daily Drilling Reports. Planning: transformer activity classification (EN/ES), Monte Carlo time forecasting, genetic-algorithm sequence prediction, Well Risk Index, hierarchical AFE/P&L aggregation. Execution: generative AI agents that mine offsets, rank hypotheses and recommend remediation; location-specific Optimum Fishing Time; GenAI end-of-well report drafting.

Results

Actively used by 250+ engineers (38% YoY growth) supporting $10–17B annual tenders. Classification 8h→20min; offset coverage = full dataset; data prep −56%; risk assessment 26× faster; a MENA tender with 56 architectures / 158 sections done in ≥½ prior time; EOWR 4 days→2 days; ~$34.7M/yr estimated value from automated technology identification. P10 time targets now consistently actionable.

Lessons

Multi-engine architecture tied to real DDR text beats a single chat UI. Keep planning (estimate/risk) and execution (NPT agents) in one ecosystem so tender assumptions feed live decisions.

Pitch for bp

Wells/drilling organisations already drown in DDRs and offset reviews. Pitch a tender+execution pilot: activity classification + probabilistic AFE on one campaign, then NPT remediation agents on active wells. KPIs: hours per well classified, offset coverage, time-to-first remediation recommendation, EOWR cycle time.

Case study 03 · SPE 235583, ADIPEC, 2 Nov 2026

AI Engineering · Tools + agents

End-To-End Petrophysical Operations with Agentic AI: Horizontal Carbonate Reservoir Well, Abu Dhabi

9configuration-driven skills
~½ day → ~5 minplanning
~90 min → <4 minlog email to deck, cached
5–10 mincold start

Problem

Petrophysics handoffs across specialists, vendor software and email from planning through drilling to completion. Slow cycles hurt geosteering decisions on heterogeneous carbonate wells.

Approach

In-house agentic assistant with nine skills in two suites. Planning: well-data registry, document compilation, logging-program generation, cement-bond evaluation, logging-activity dashboard. Operations: vendor-aware quick-look interpretation, production-log checklist, drilling-correspondence router, inbox triage. Skills chain deterministically (geosteering tops seed per-zone Archie parameters). Porosity via density-neutron with sonic fallback across two contractors’ runs.

Results

On Well A (confidential): planning package ~5 minutes vs typical half-day. Cached log→deck <4 minutes; cold start 5–10 minutes. Automated subzone tagging tracked lithology change within the same shift. Cement-bond and PL prep completed the shift source data arrived. Output checked against independent commercial-software interpretation.

Lessons

Reliability came from deterministic engineering under the language model, not free-form LLM petrophysics. Configuration-driven skills beat one mega-prompt. Keep vendor-awareness and dual-contractor assembly explicit.

Pitch for bp

Subsurface and petrophysics teams live the email-and-vendor-software loop. Propose a nine-skill style assistant for one development well campaign: auto-parse daily geosteering → zone parameters → quick-look deck, with commercial-software parity as the acceptance gate. KPI: minutes from log arrival to steering-ready deck; SME override rate.

Case study 04 · McKinsey, 23 Sep 2026

Maintenance Meets AI: A Proven Approach for Asset-Heavy Industries

~2–5 ptsincremental margin, indicative
~10–15%maintenance conversion-cost opportunity
~6 mo → 6 wkUS O&G FMEA/PM site effort, ~80%
~8%less maintenance work volume
AOG −14%airline inventory −12%; 2.5× case

Problem

Maintenance is one of the largest controllable cost buckets in energy, mining, airlines and rail, yet is often treated as a cost centre sitting on underused work-order and reliability data.

Approach

End-to-end rewiring across reliability, planning/execution, turnarounds, spares and contractors — not algorithm shopping. Examples: gen-AI workflows that update asset hierarchies, draft FMEAs from work history and PM plans from P&IDs; reliability agents that synthesise bad actors; agentic scheduling; risk-ranked turnaround scope.

Results

US oil and gas company: ~80% less time to complete FMEA/PM work for one site (six months → six weeks). Reliability agent: ~8% reduction in total maintenance work volume. Airline transformation: AOG −14%, inventory −12%, ~10% of maintenance P&L unlocked, impact 2.5× initial case. Packaging manufacturer: +85% technician productivity via agentic scheduling. Mine: 3× mean time between failures with frontline troubleshooting agents.

Lessons

Value comes from embedding AI in reliability/planner/technician routines and rewiring end-to-end so bottlenecks do not just move. Six rewiring principles: ambitious goals, accountable teams, incremental data products, API platform, agile operating model, adoption-first change.

Pitch for bp

Pair this with the UptimeAI pattern. Pitch a reliability-engineer productivity pilot: gen-AI FMEA/PM drafting on one equipment class + a bad-actor agent, measured on weeks-to-complete-site and maintenance work volume. Frame McKinsey’s 2–5 pt margin range as the executive narrative, the 80% site-time cut as the local proof.

Case study 05 · Google Cloud / KDDI, 8 Sep 2026

AI Engineering · Context

How KDDI Optimized RAG Performance with Agent Development Kit

−38%total response latency
~−18%TTFT
+25%groundedness
75%less eval workload via sampling
>800 linessystem prompt split into ADK Skills

Problem

Buffmee, KDDI’s consumer RAG app grounded in 100+ books/magazines/web sources, missed latency targets and needed hallucination-free answers across diverse media types.

Approach

Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform Evaluation Service with LLM-as-a-Judge and “Rule of Hundreds”; binary pass/fail for critical metrics; content sampling on a format × media-composition grid (75% eval cut); product-owner-calibrated thresholds; BigQuery Agent Analytics + ADK log-analysis agent to find skill-division and prompt-bloat bottlenecks; modular ADK Skills loaded dynamically instead of one massive prompt.

Results

−38% total latency (hit target), ~−18% TTFT, +25% groundedness. Shipped a trustworthy personalised Q&A experience for content providers.

Lessons

Measure groundedness and latency as first-class product metrics. Giant system prompts cause attention drift and TTFT pain — split into skills. Binary evals + human-calibrated thresholds beat noisy 1–5 scores for ship/no-ship.

Pitch for bp

Any internal knowledge assistant (procedures, MOCs, engineering standards) needs the same loop. Propose an ADK-style (or LangGraph/Crew skill) refactor of an existing copilot: split the mega-prompt, add LLM-as-judge groundedness on a held-out set, and report latency + groundedness before/after. Bonus talking point from Elastic’s 1M-message production study: partial/tangential retrieval scored worse (8.15/10) than retrieving nothing (9.18/10) — set a relevance floor and prefer “I don’t know”.

For the week

Consulting playbook

Challenge the alert before you act on it. The strongest energy pattern this week is counterfactual diagnosis (UptimeAI) plus deterministic domain logic under the LLM (petrophysics skills, well-construction engines), not chat-on-top-of-documents. When you pitch inside bp: (1) pick one asset class or workflow, (2) define the decision the agent must improve (shutdown vs continue, log-to-deck minutes, hours per well classified), (3) put consistency checks / operating envelopes / commercial-software parity in front of autonomy, (4) report a before/after metric executives already care about ($ avoided, hours, uplift). McKinsey’s maintenance article is your board-level narrative; the SPE and UptimeAI pieces are your proof packs.

Hands-on

Framework trend worth learning hands-on

ADK-style Skills + retrieval confidence floors. Rebuild a small internal RAG agent so tools/prompts are modular Skills (dynamically loaded) rather than one 800-line system prompt, and retrieval returns nothing below a relevance threshold. Benchmark: TTFT, end-to-end latency, groundedness (LLM-as-judge, binary), and a “trick set” of partial-context queries where the wrong-but-similar doc should be refused. Use Google ADK if convenient, or mirror the pattern in LangGraph / Pydantic AI / CrewAI skills. Elastic’s production finding is the acceptance test: partial context must not beat “I don’t know”.

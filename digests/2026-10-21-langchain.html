Case study · Healthcare

Numan’s eval-first AI health coach, Nu, on LangGraph and LangSmith

Numan built an eval-first AI health coach, Nu, using LangGraph and LangSmith. Nu doesn't diagnose or prescribe; it coaches, and it must not cross the medical device line at any point.

Their solution is an eval-first approach: every eval runs through the same graph patients talk to, scored per category, with simulated patients played by LLM personas. The Numan team's blog post covers how they simulate and score these conversations, how they hold the regulatory line, how they ship sub-agents in weeks and migrate frontier models in just a day, and what the architecture looks like.

Why it matters

A concrete pattern for regulated domains: test the production graph itself with simulated users and per-category scores, so compliance is measured rather than assumed. Model migrations in a day are the payoff of that eval suite.

Workshop · Toronto, 3 Nov

Build agents your security team will approve, with Arcade.dev

On Tuesday, November 3rd, LangChain and Arcade.dev host a half-day workshop in Toronto.

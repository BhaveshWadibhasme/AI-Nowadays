PhD Fellowship · about 13 Nov

148 new fellows in the 2026 Google PhD Fellowship

The 2026 Google PhD Fellowship Program named 148 new PhD fellows from 38 countries, supported by $13 million from Google.org. Since 2009 the program has supported more than 1,100 graduate students. The research spans energy-efficient chip design, quantum experiments, reliable reasoning in language models, interpretable medical imaging, and speech tech for underrepresented languages.

Open-source genomics · about 13 Nov

Open-source genomics for Australian giant kelp, with CSIRO

With CSIRO, work to revive Australian giant kelp forests published two near-perfect reference genomes. 600 wild kelp samples across 40 sites are mapping genetic diversity and heat-tolerance markers. Heat-tolerant juveniles are being cross-bred for future pilot tests. The genomic tools were released open-source.

Why it matters

Two near-perfect reference genomes, 600 wild kelp samples across 40 sites, and open-source genomic tools are how this CSIRO collaboration maps genetic diversity and heat-tolerance markers, and cross-breeds heat-tolerant juveniles for future pilot tests.

Wearables · about 12 Nov

Passive wearable signals, translated into a coachable health age

A blog describes an investigational ML framework that translates passive wearable signals into a modifiable, coachable health age. It uses an action-conditioned world model to simulate personalized 12-week "what-if" habit trajectories. The work expands to routine blood biomarkers to track early cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic (CKM) health.

Why it matters

The investigational framework uses an action-conditioned world model to simulate personalized 12-week "what-if" habit trajectories from passive wearable signals, and expands to routine blood biomarkers for early cardiovascular-kidney-metabolic health.

Health evaluation · about 9 to 10 Nov

AI Engineering · Measure

A whitepaper on evaluating generative AI for health

Reposted from Google for Health, a whitepaper describes a multi-method framework for evaluating generative AI for health. It combines closed-question benchmarks, automated LLM-as-a-judge assessments, and expert human review to evaluate accuracy, safety, and usefulness, across the lifecycle from development to post-deployment monitoring.

Singapore · about 10 Nov

Notes from a week in Singapore

Google Research Singapore launched.

Case study 01 · SPE ATCE 2026

AI Engineering · Measure

Building an Evaluation Harness for Oil & Gas AI Agents (Occidental, SPE ATCE 2026)

63%pass@5
48%pass@1
37%pass5
19SME-curated tasks

Problem

Occidental runs 100+ agents on an internal agent platform, but agent evaluation in oil and gas usually checks only final-answer accuracy and ignores consistency and efficiency.

Approach

An evaluation suite for DrillingGPT (a drilling-data agent) with 19 SME-curated tasks of varying difficulty. Each task was run 5 times to compute pass@k (succeeds at least once) and passk (succeeds every time), plus tool-call precision, recall and redundancy. Traces were reviewed to find failure modes.

Results

pass@5 of 63%, pass@1 of 48%, pass5 of 37%. The agent can often reach the right answer but not reliably. Easy and medium tasks were OK; hard tasks dropped sharply on every metric. Tool use was conservative (high precision, lower recall).

Lessons

Failure modes were partial errors in multi-well answers, compounding mistakes in multi-table aggregations, failing to refuse out-of-scope requests, and skipping necessary tools. A single accuracy number hides the consistency gap.

Pitch for bp

Before any internal agent or copilot goes wider, propose a small SME-curated task set (15-25 tasks) run k times, reporting passk and tool-call recall. It's cheap, gives leadership a reliability number, and is a strong Forward Deployed Engineer-style deliverable.

Case study 02 · SPE ATCE 2026

AI Engineering · Tools + agents

A Multi-Agent AI Optimization System for Rig Utilization in the Burgan Field (SPE ATCE 2026)

+16%projected 90-day production gain
-46%fleet-average rig idle time
Minutesschedule prep, from about ten days

Problem

Scheduling about 40 workover rigs across roughly 200-500 wells a year in the Greater Burgan complex was done by hand in an in-house system with around ten stakeholder teams, under many constraints (artificial-lift readiness, materials, site access, rig-well compatibility).

Approach

Cooperating agents for data preprocessing, rig-well matching, constraint validation, schedule optimization (a genetic algorithm tuned to the operator's rules, with short- and long-term memory), execution monitoring and feedback. Human-in-the-loop review and approval kept throughout. Planners choose “maximize production gain” or “meet a target”, and stability rules protect active and next-scheduled jobs.

Results

Against manual schedules on identical inputs, plus 90-day rolling backtests: projected 90-day production gain +16%, fleet-average rig idle time -46%, schedule preparation from about ten days to minutes. (Volumes normalized for confidentiality.)

Lessons

The “agents” wrap a classical optimizer; the value comes from encoding the constraint taxonomy and keeping planners in control. Stability rules matter so the plan doesn't churn.

Pitch for bp

Look for planning problems run by spreadsheets and meetings (maintenance turnarounds, crew or vessel scheduling, logistics). Pitch “agent orchestration around a proven optimizer with human sign-off”, and measure against the manual baseline with backtesting.

Case study 03 · SPE ATCE 2026

AI Engineering · Guardrails

A Physics-Informed, Retrieval-Augmented Industrial Agent for Offshore Gas Networks (SPE ATCE 2026)

39/40outputs fully or partially useful
37of 40 finalizations adequate
~90%faster notifications

Problem

Offshore gas pipeline networks generate constant measurements, alarms, records and external-event signals; the bottleneck is turning a candidate signal into a useful technical assessment.

Approach

A RAG agent combining contextual knowledge, approved data-access tools, physical-consistency guidance and a swappable LLM adapter, in a governed asynchronous workflow. It gives evidence-based decision support while humans keep operational authority.

Results

Evaluated on a frozen 10.5-day window of 403 consecutive work items. In a 40-case expert review, 39 outputs were fully or partially useful and finalization was adequate in 37. In eight matched event pairs, notification latency fell about 90% versus the conventional communication path.

Lessons

Productivity and workload were not quantified. Performance was sensitive to source and tool availability, trigger coverage, measurement observability, endpoint integration, configuration quality and premature finalization.

Pitch for bp

A strong template for an operations “signal to assessment” assistant: physics checks as guardrails, approved tools only, a model adapter so you aren't locked in, and an evaluation on a frozen window before go-live.

Case study 04 · SPE ATCE 2026

AI Engineering · Guardrails

AI-Enabled Digital Twins for Integrated Production Optimization (ADNOC AiPSO, SPE ATCE 2026)

1-2%sustained production uplift
Noneincremental capex
8producing fields, phase 1
25+digital workflows

Problem

Legacy surveillance and delayed diagnostics across thousands of wells, multiple networks and reservoirs.

Approach

An integrated production-system digital twin (reservoir, wells, surface) built on hybrid physics and AI models, on an industrial data and AI foundation with a central DataOps pipeline. GenAI progresses from an engineer copilot to domain advisory services to an AiPSO Agent. Real-time engineering operating envelopes define safe limits and enable autonomous well control.

Results

Phase 1 across eight producing fields in 2025-2026, with 25+ digital workflows, gave a sustained 1-2% production uplift with no incremental capex, plus earlier detection of constraints and shorter time-to-intervention.

Lessons

The GenAI layer sits on top of years of data foundation work; autonomy is bounded by operating envelopes. Staged copilot, then advisor, then agent is the maturity path.

Pitch for bp

Use it to argue that GenAI value in production depends on the data foundation, and propose the staged autonomy ladder with explicit safe-operating envelopes as the guardrail.

Case study 05 · Gartner, 19 Oct 2026

AI Engineering · Measure

Gartner: Only 13% of CIOs Say AI Has Delivered Significant Value (19 Oct 2026)

13%significant or transformational value
2.3xmore likely vanguards with stop rules
2.6xwith rules to retire low-value AI
2,002CIOs and tech executives

Problem

AI use cases are multiplying, but the chance of a use case succeeding is still about one in five.

Approach

Survey of 2,002 CIOs and tech executives (May-July 2026) identifying “vanguards” who deliver better business outcomes and high CEO-perceived AI value.

Results

Only 13% report significant or transformational value. Organizations with rules for stopping pilots before production are 2.3x more likely to be vanguards, and with rules for retiring low-value production AI 2.6x, yet only 31% and 17% have them. 58% feel more pressure to deliver cost improvement; 51% expect AI to raise total cost of ownership. Only 4% have plans to redesign roles for a human-agent workforce (2.4x more likely vanguards). Related: Gartner predicts 40% of agentic AI projects will be canceled by end of 2027, and BCG's Applied AI Index 2026 finds companies with all six AI controls get 3x the agentic value.

Lessons

“Volume is not value.” AI costs are probabilistic and set at run time, so old funding models don't fit.

Pitch for bp

Bring a one-page use-case intake with value defined up front, explicit kill criteria at pilot and production, and run-time cost tracking. That makes you the person who helps the department stop the wrong projects early.

For the week

Consulting playbook

Define value and kill criteria before you build, measure reliability (passk, not one accuracy number) before you grant autonomy, and climb the autonomy ladder (copilot, advisor, bounded agent) inside hard engineering guardrails. The winning oil and gas cases this week all kept a human approver and wrapped agents around proven physics or optimization methods.

Hands-on

Framework trend worth learning hands-on

Agent evaluation harnesses: build a small eval set for a LangGraph agent and score it with pass@k / passk and tool-call precision and recall, using LangSmith evaluations or an open tool like Arize Phoenix. Re-create the Occidental-style harness on a toy drilling-data or maintenance-log agent this weekend.

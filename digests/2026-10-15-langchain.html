Deep Agents

How do agents remember?

The longer an agent runs, the more it takes in (instructions, tool results, files, and drafts), which results in decreased accuracy and reasoning. LangChain's Deep Agents harness addresses this by moving context out of the active window without deleting it.

Why it matters

Long-running agents need memory that stays out of the prompt window so reasoning stays sharp as jobs stretch.

Case study

Orange Business built Live Intelligence on LangChain and LangSmith

Orange Business's flagship enterprise AI platform, Live Intelligence, runs across 120,000 employees and 150+ customers in France and Europe. The team chose LangChain for model neutrality and portability, and use LangSmith for observability, a three-layer evaluation setup, and regression evals before changes reach production. Mathieu Ducrot, Head of AI Products: before LangSmith, debugging was hard because failures were hidden inside model behavior; with it, engineers can inspect each step and tell whether the issue comes from retrieval, the prompt, the model, the tool call, or the business logic. The write-up says LangChain and LangSmith saved them 12–18 months of development.

Why it matters

A large telco case study that ties LangSmith tracing and layered evals to shipping enterprise agents faster.

Evals · Interrupt London

monday.com's hybrid eval setup

Dor Cohen (AI Engineering Director, monday.com) shared their hybrid evaluation playbook from Interrupt London. Offline evals run before production against datasets with expected outcomes and answer "Can we ship?" Online evals analyze real production traces and answer "Is it working for real users?" They track Task Completion, Trajectory Health (right tools, efficient execution), and User Frustration, and break conversations down by domain, family, and use case while measuring quality, latency, and cost. The loop: Production → Detect failures → Offline tests → Fix → Verify in production. "LLM judges aren't the ground truth" — trust comes from human reviews, comparing judges, repeated scoring, and user feedback. The real value of evals isn't knowing your agent's score; it's knowing what to improve next.

Why it matters

A concrete offline/online eval split you can copy for FDE and internal agent rollouts.
